## What this file is

The one place where every decision of Eve has a NUMBER that a script can read (`D-0001`, `D-0002`, ...). A brief quotes numbers from here (`.claude/workflows/decision_compliance.md`); the hook `.claude/hooks/coordinator_guard.py` refuses a builder or a reviewer call whose brief cites a number that does not exist; `assets/scripts/decision_ledger.py` reads this file (`list`, `check D-0001 ...`).

## Line format

One decision per line, ten fields separated by " | " (a line that starts with `D-` and a four-digit number is a decision; every other line is text):

`number | date | area | decision | source | taken by | status | relation | her words and evidence | old key`

- **taken by**: `EVE` = she decided or reviewed it, with the record quoted. `AGENT` = an agent or the coordinator chose it. `NOT STATED` = the record gives a decision without saying who took it. `REVIEW SESSION` = a plan that the review session relayed in the agent channel and that she did not say in her own words. Nothing is assumed.
- **status**: only a line taken by EVE is `DECIDED`. An AGENT line is `PROVISIONAL` (it goes back to her). A NOT STATED line is `NOT-STATED` (not a decision until she confirms it). A REVIEW SESSION line is `RELAYED` (a plan, not decided until she confirms it; it names the decided line it was split from). The script refuses a line whose status does not follow its "taken by".
- **relation**: `-` when the record names none. A later decision names the number it replaces (`replaces D-NNNN`, `replaced in part by D-NNNN`, `refines`, `refined by`, `consistent with`). A reversal is also a decision and gets its own line.
- **old key**: the key of the line in the earlier ledger (`L01`...`L47`, `N01`...`N52`) or the channel block of the new lines (`M140-a`): the texts of the old lines still name old keys (for example "replaced by N01"); this field maps them to the number.
- Dates are written in full (`2026-11-05`); the old ledger wrote `11-05`.
- **Who writes a line**: a scribe, the same day the decision is taken, with her words quoted. Nobody rewrites an existing line; a change of mind is a new line that names the one it replaces. (One exception, 2026-11-16, on the review of this file: `D-0108` to `D-0129` were rewritten once so that each holds only her words; the parts that were the review session's own moved to `D-0130` to `D-0142`; a number was never reused for another text. A second exception, 2026-11-16 (Eve, `D-0161`): a relayed line that she then confirmed changed its taken by, status and relation only (the relation ends with "; confirmed by D-0161"; `D-0140` ends with "; replaced by D-0141 (recorded by D-0161)"); its decision words and every other field did not change. `app/tests/test_decision_ledger.py` allows exactly this and nothing else.)
