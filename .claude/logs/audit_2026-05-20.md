### Decisions (ADR-082, with Eve)

- **Q1 preview** = on-Apply (ratified). **Q2 T3** = manifest-only (BLUEPRINT never authors T3 — hard invariant).
  **Q4 comments** = lost on form round-trip (documented limitation, MVP). **Q5 join** = dedicated Joint Designer pane.
  **Q6 group/plot** = sidebar inventory (MVP) → context menu (v2). **Q7 inspection** = glimpse only.
- **NEW: manifest-draft autosave** (BP-AUTOSAVE-1) — persist in-progress draft + undo history, modeled on HOME ghost-save.
- **AI assistant** = advise-only, never blocking, Socratic nudging on bad practice. Definition file already exists
  (`config/ui/agents/blueprint_default.md`).
- **Q3 branch (the key terminology fix)** — **branch = lineage bifurcation at a node**: upstream shared
  (Tier 1 trunk materialized once, branches `scan_parquet` it), child lineage diverges downstream as a
  fragment-per-component `!include` (canonical `Summary` / `Summary_quality`). This is the Bifurcation Point
  Rule made interactive. NOT whole-manifest duplication (rare, new-data-only). The TubeMap graph *fan-out* is the
  visual; the *manifest branch* is the lineage split — Eve and I were using "branch" for different things.

  the "separate files" she meant are the fragment-per-component `!include` files, not duplicate manifests.
  Reading `config/manifests/pipelines/1_test_data_ST22_dummy.yaml` and its fragment tree resolved it.
