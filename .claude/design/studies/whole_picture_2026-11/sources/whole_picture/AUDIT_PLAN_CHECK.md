Say in the plan which of two ways: (a) extend `run_audit`/`PlotRun` to keep the two frames (edit of a tested file, `app/tests/test_audit_run.py`), or (b) `audit_effects.py` calls `orch.materialize_tier3(..., loaded=loaded_from_resolved(...), tier2_path=...)` itself for the original and the audited plot (signature `orchestrator.py:690`; the keys and paths are those the run used, and the folders of the run, which the plan does not name either).
