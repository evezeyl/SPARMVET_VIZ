## Slice A: the groundwork (nothing a user sees changes)

- **For the user:** nothing changes on screen. HOME behaves as before.
- **What exists at its end:** (1) one description of a recipe as a map (which table feeds which join and which plot), in the shared library; Blueprint's tube map and the audit will both read it. (2) The engine reads tier 3 at the plot entry (today the checker refuses a non-empty tier 3). (3) A key per stored table, so that a change in one table rebuilds only what depends on it (today one key covers the whole recipe). (4) The audit recipe: a copy of the recipe in which a step can be written at the end of a tier, a run without screen that builds its tables.
- **What it replaces in the old code:** the single key per recipe in `app/modules/cache_keys.py` and its use in `app/modules/orchestrator.py`; the refusal of tier 3 in the checker (`libs/utils/.../recipe_check.py`); nothing of the audit screens yet.
- **Tests:** written first: every shape of recipe gives the right map (no join, one join, a join of joins, a table used twice, a plot on a table, a plot on a join); the copy without audit steps gives exactly the original's tables (the golden tables); a step at tier 1, 2 and 3 changes only the tables after it; a changed table rebuilds only its dependants; the pictures of all shipped recipes stay identical.
- **Risk:** the key per table touches every stored table and the export's key check; a mistake here could serve a stale table. Guard: the keys are tested table by table, and the cache version changes so nothing old is served.
- **Builds on:** D-0102, D-0135, D-0143, D-0148 (the audit recipe, the copy); D-0146 (steps at the end of each tier); D-0240 and D-0003 (the keys; today's step 6); ADR-101 (a key per table, the design).
- **Needs first:** the big recipe change merged (the map and the copy read the new tier shape).
- **Open:** point 9 above.
- **Size:** about 6 pieces or more (a piece = about one hour of agent work, measured today), plus reviews and one or two full control runs.

## Slice C: the audit itself, "Create audit step"

- **For the user:** "Create audit step" opens the decided dialog: every table and plot the step would reach, each with a tick (all ticked at opening); for each, the rows and samples before and after; plain warnings (a later step summarises rows; a place would become empty); a required reason; Apply and Cancel. Apply takes effect; Cancel changes nothing. No "Pending", no second Apply. Each place of a step has a status: applied, not concerned, or broken; a broken step is shown and blocks the export; nothing is skipped in silence. "Remove these samples from all tables" as the one automatic placement. The view of the tables at tier 1, 2 and 3 with the audit steps; the recipe overview in the right panel; remove a step (and the undo controls of point 2).
- **What it replaces in the old code:** the dialog "Apply to: This plot only / All plots / All except" (`filter_and_audit_handlers.py:853-957`); "Send to Audit" (`:511, 629-687`); the "Pending" list and the second Apply of the audit panel (`audit_stack.py`); the store of audit steps per plot (`home_state`, keyed by plot), replaced by the audit recipe of slice A. The join-key block repaired today (D-0297) is kept as a rule and re-expressed in the new dialog as a warning or a block, as decided (D-0095, D-0168: signals of the audit are warnings; the key drop stays refused unless she says otherwise).
- **Tests:** written first: the numbers before and after equal what the audit recipe produces (a concordance test on every shipped recipe); Cancel writes nothing; a step with no reason cannot be applied; a broken step blocks the export; "remove from all tables" reaches every table that holds the sample key; removing a step restores the tables; a restored session shows the same steps and numbers.
- **Risk:** the largest slice. The numbers must be honest: they come from running the audit recipe, not from a second calculation. Time: counting rows on every place for every step can be slow on large data; to measure on the largest shipped recipe before the dialog is finished.
- **Builds on:** D-0016, D-0023, D-0026, D-0093, D-0194, D-0168, D-0095, D-0146, D-0302 (today: a step is saved only when applied with a reason).
- **Needs first:** slices A and B; answers 2, 4, 8.
- **Size:** about 8 to 9 pieces.
