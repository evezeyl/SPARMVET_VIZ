## S11. The three cautions of 'an earlier place covers the later ones'

### Caution 1: in one chain (the same table: T1 -> T2 -> the plots that read it), a step at T1 gives the same rows at the later places as ticking all of them
- ids (21 of the T2 view): tables that have the column at T1: 27; step at T1 only = ticking T1 + T2 + plots at every later place (exact frames): 27 of 27; differ: none
- country != Norway: tables that have the column at T1: 14; step at T1 only = ticking T1 + T2 + plots at every later place (exact frames): 14 of 14; differ: none
- year != 2023: tables that have the column at T1: 11; step at T1 only = ticking T1 + T2 + plots at every later place (exact frames): 11 of 11; differ: none
(Later places that lack the column cannot be ticked: they are not in the comparison; they still receive the effect from T1.)

### Caution 2: only when the column is unchanged in between
Real cases of recipe 1 (from S4): 
- same rows, different VALUES in a derived column: QA_population_zscore (n50_zscore differs for 14 of 14 remaining samples, largest difference 0.5694); ST22_Anchor (virulence_count differs on 1543 of 1543 rows). The rows removed are the same at T1 and at T2; the numbers computed in between are not.
- a rename: VirulenceFinder `VF_gene` = ST22_Anchor `Selected_Virulence_Genes` = AR1 `selected_virulence_genes`: the same rows are removed at T1 of the anchor by either name (identical tables), but a search by the name finds only 4 of the places that hold it.
- a derived value: `era` exists only at T2; `year_label` only from QA_year_types on; a condition on them has no earlier place.
- SCRATCH copy (not in config/): one extra step at the start of MLST_with_metadata tier2 recodes `country` 'Norway' -> 'Norge' (a recode between the two places). T1 shows 28 Norway samples; T2 shows 21 Norge samples and 0 Norway.
    The user selects `country = Norge` at the T2 view (21 samples) and the rule writes the SAME condition at the earliest place T1: T1 rows 42 -> 42 (removed 0); T2 rows 33 -> 33. Written at T2: T2 rows 33 -> 12.
    The condition `country != Norway` (the T1 name): at T1 42 -> 14 rows, T2 33 -> 12; at T2 only: T2 33 -> 33 (removes nothing: the value is already 'Norge').
    By ids (the 21 ids read at the T2 view) written at T1: T1 42 -> 21, T2 33 -> 12. The ids survive the recode; the property does not.

### Caution 3: places of other chains are not covered
Step `country != Norway` at metadata_schema@T1. Places downstream by the graph that hold sample ids: 38; the 28 Norway samples are gone from 22 of them and still there at 16: J:AR1_MLST_Serotype_Virulence@T1, J:AR1_MLST_Serotype_Virulence@T2, J:Bracken_with_metadata@T1, J:Bracken_with_metadata@T2, J:FastP_with_metadata@T1, J:FastP_with_metadata@T2, J:MLST_with_metadata@T1, J:QC_Reads_Anchor@T1, J:QC_Reads_Anchor@T2, J:Quast_with_metadata@T1, J:Quast_with_metadata@T2, J:ResFinder_with_metadata@T1, J:ResFinder_with_metadata@T2, P:assembly_quality_dotplot@T3, P:miaou@T3, P:qc_reads_horizontal_barplot@T3
Places NOT downstream (other chains) that hold sample ids: 30; they still hold the Norway samples at 30 of them (unchanged: True).
So 'the root covers the lineage' holds at 22 + the root itself; it fails at 16 downstream places (metadata is the lookup side of a left join: the samples stay with the value empty) and at 30 places of other chains.
Ticking EVERY place that has `country` with the same property step (na keep) leaves the Norway samples at 16 downstream places (the earlier tick blanks `country` in the lookup tables; the later tick sees an empty value and, with na: keep, keeps the row): J:AR1_MLST_Serotype_Virulence@T1, J:AR1_MLST_Serotype_Virulence@T2, J:Bracken_with_metadata@T1, J:Bracken_with_metadata@T2, J:FastP_with_metadata@T1, J:FastP_with_metadata@T2, J:MLST_with_metadata@T1, J:QC_Reads_Anchor@T1 ...
With na: remove at every place: the Norway samples remain at 0 places. But na remove also removes any sample whose value was empty from the start (here none; the recipe's own data has no empty country: 0 rows).
