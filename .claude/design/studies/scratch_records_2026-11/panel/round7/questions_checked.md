## B. The two export modes

**What the records say, in plain sentences.**

- ADR-097 (2026-11-09, "Decided by Eve", `architecture_decisions.md:4806-4980`) defines two export modes that can be combined in one zip. Eve corrected the first draft herself: mode 1 must never hold the recipe or the raw data, because "the point is for the user to provide evidence that its the same and where things come from" (lines 4857-4859).
- Mode 1 is "Publication": plots, report, data tables, the audit record `t3_steps.yaml`, and `effective_run.yaml`. Mode 2 is "Reproducibility": it adds the recipe and the real data files. Both modes carry the same `effective_run.yaml` (lines 4865-4880).
- The decision of 2026-11-12 for audit mode (P4b, `home_audit_steps.md:346`) keeps one bundle and one tick box "include recipe and raw data", off by default. The tick adds the recipe (in audit mode the forked recipe) and the raw data files, together.
- The audit steps are always in the bundle in audit mode: the steps with their reasons, the attrition table, `exclusions.tsv`, the history log, the original plot beside the audited one (P4b, `home_audit_steps.md:346`).

**How the three names relate (READ).**
- "Reproducibility bundle" is the name of mode 2: the zip folders `data_analysis_recipes_reproducibility/` and `data_reproducibility/` (ADR-097 L4876-4880). It is the choice behind the box.
- "Forked recipe" is the recipe inside that bundle. Today it is built per plot: a join is copied, the plot's steps are appended, the plot is pointed to the copy (ADR-097 point 4; `t3_translator.py:35-37`; RUN by the evidence file: a new join `J__t3_p1` is added and the plot is repointed).
- "Audit recipe" is, in `home_audit_steps.md:132`, the same thing: "the original recipe plus audit steps ... (the forked recipe, ADR-097/099)". The target form is a copy of the whole recipe (`home_audit_steps.md:164`). It is not built (`home_stage2_mechanism_spec.md:26-33`).
