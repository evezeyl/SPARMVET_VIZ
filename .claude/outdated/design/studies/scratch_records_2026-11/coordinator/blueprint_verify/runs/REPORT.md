Cause: `str(current_val or "")` makes 0 an empty box, and an empty box is not saved.

CONFIRMED. `scale_color_manual values: [red, blue]` (and the dict `{x: red, y: blue}`) -> after Apply `values: "['red', 'blue']"` (a text; also `guide: legend, drop: false, na_translate: false` added).
