# Sources check: EUCAST and microbiology claims of the 34 Gallery examples

Date: 2026-11-21. Decision quoted: D-0401 "ALL 34 recipes are verified critically".
Method: grep of the 34 `example_meta.md`, the recipes' `info` texts and the four viewpoint reports; each claim looked up in a primary document that was downloaded and read as text (pdftotext). Nothing answered from memory. No file of the repository was changed.

## Sources read

- S1. EUCAST, "Expected Resistant Phenotypes", Version 1.2, January 2023. https://www.eucast.org/fileadmin/eucast/pdf/expert_rules/Expected_Resistant_Phenotypes_v1.2_20230113.pdf (linked from https://eucast.org/expert_rules_and_expected_phenotypes pages; the PDF was read in full text, Table 4 "Intrinsic resistance in gram-positive bacteria", row 4.7).
- S2. EUCAST, "Intrinsic Resistance and Unusual Phenotypes Tables", v3.3, 2021-10-18 (the predecessor of S1). Read from a copy at https://git.web.rug.nl/P281424/AMR/raw/tag/v1.8.1/data-raw/Intrinsic_Resistance_and_Unusual_Phenotypes_Tables_v3.3_20211018.pdf (third-party copy; the content is the same table as S1).
- S3. EUCAST, "Breakpoint tables for interpretation of MICs and zone diameters", Version 16.1, valid from 2026-06-24. https://www.eucast.org/fileadmin/eucast/pdf/breakpoints/v_16.1_Breakpoint_Tables.pdf (read in full text; Notes sheet, Enterococcus spp. sheet, the Enterobacterales sheet).

How the E. faecalis row was read: the column headings of the table are rotated text, so the column of each "R" was found from the x position of the words (pdftotext -bbox). In S1 and S2 the row "4.7 Enterococcus faecalis" has an "R" at the x position of the heading "Cephalosporins (except ceftazidime)" and another "R" under "Ceftazidime". So both texts list E. faecalis as resistant to cephalosporins.

## Where the example data are random or invented

- The common test table's `mic_value` is random uniform 0 to 127 (said in the table for the owner; not re-checked here). The same holds for the other Gallery tables with species and MIC (`freqpoly`, `ecdf`, `beeswarm_sina`, `qq_plot`, `violin_boxplot`, `error_bar`, `density_2d_bin`): their guidance texts say "MIC" and "isolates" but never say "invented values". The question for them is only whether the text presents the numbers as real; see rows 6 to 11.
- `bar_grouped`, `step_chart`: the numbers are fixed (read in the repository), their origin is not stated in the texts.

## Corrections this would mean for the table for the owner (facts, not edits)

1. Section 8 says the E. faecalis claim is "the agents' own knowledge, not looked up". It is now looked up: EUCAST lists E. faecalis (row 4.7) as resistant to cephalosporins in the "Intrinsic Resistance and Unusual Phenotypes" v3.3 (2021) and in "Expected Resistant Phenotypes" v1.2 (2023). The statement holds for the species in the data; EUCAST has dropped the word "intrinsic" since 2023, so a page should say "expected resistant phenotype".
2. Section 8 says the claim that EUCAST publishes breakpoints per drug and species is external knowledge. It is confirmed by v16.1 (2026-06-24): each row is one agent, with S≤ and R>.
3. The step_chart title and y label "EUCAST ... MIC Breakpoint (mg/L)" with one value per drug class do not match any EUCAST table (v16.1). Whether the numbers equal historical EUCAST values stays not verified.
4. The ecdf sentence "reading off %S, %I, %R directly from breakpoints" does not say that the breakpoints are per agent and species. In v16.1 the category "I" is "Susceptible, increased exposure".
5. In the Enterococcus sheet of v16.1 every cephalosporin has "-": EUCAST gives no clinical breakpoint for them. A resistance rate for E. faecalis x cephalosporins therefore has no EUCAST breakpoint behind it either; the 0.0 value is not "wrong because of a breakpoint", it is unexpected because of the expected resistant phenotype.
6. The tables with `mic_value` random 0 to 127: their pages call the column "MIC" without saying that the values are invented. This is a fact about the text, not an EUCAST claim.

## Not verified

- Whether the step_chart values (0.125 to 1.0 mg/L, 2014 to 2023) match any real EUCAST breakpoint at any date: the old versions of the breakpoint tables were not read.
- That MIC values come as a two-fold dilution series (ISO 20776-1 not read), and the sentences on log-transformation of MIC, on cross-resistance, on clonal spread and antibiotic use, and on the 95 % interval of a linear fit.
- Any claim in the example tables about real resistance rates (the common tables are random or invented; no external data were compared).
- The Enterobacterales rows (ciprofloxacin 0.25 and 0.5, meropenem 2 and 8) were read from the extracted text of S3 and attributed to the Enterobacterales sheet by position (before the Pseudomonas sheet); the sheet heading of that block was not read on its own line.
- The v3.3 copy (S2) comes from a third-party site; the EUCAST copy of v1.2 (S1) is the primary source and says the same for row 4.7.
