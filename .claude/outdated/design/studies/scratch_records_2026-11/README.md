# Scratch records, November 2026

Copied on 2026-11-25 from `tmpAI/panel/`, `tmpAI/coordinator/`, `tmpAI/` and `tmp/`, because a ledger line, a task of `tasks.md`, a design file, an ADR or the handoff names them as the source of a decision or a task. `tmp/` and `tmpAI/` are scratch and not in git (`rules_verification_testing.md` section 6, no permanent document stays only there).

What is here: the `.md` documents (each up to 300 KB; a single named file up to 1 MB) of 655 files, in the same folder layout as the original (`panel/<topic>/...`, `coordinator/<topic>/...`, `tmpAI_...`). `MAP.tsv` gives the old and the new path of every file.

What is NOT here (left in the original folder in `tmpAI/`, listed in `tmpAI/KEEP_LIST.md`): data files, logs, pictures, scripts, parquet files, copies of repository trees, `.txt` outputs. Do not look for them here.

Nothing in this folder is a decision by itself. A decision is a line of `.claude/knowledge/decision_ledger.md` taken by the owner; these files are panel answers, reports of builders and reviewers, checks and briefs. Files that a panel or a builder wrote may contain proposals that the owner did not take.

Two groups were put in the studies they belong to: the documents of the whole-picture work in `whole_picture_2026-11/sources/` and the lineage documents in `lineage_scope_2026-11/sources/`.
