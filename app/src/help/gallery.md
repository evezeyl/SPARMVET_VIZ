## Gallery — Quick Reference

**Gallery** is a curated browser of visualization recipes — pre-built plot templates you can adapt to your data.

### Browsing
Use the **Family**, **Pattern**, **Geom**, and **Difficulty** filter chips to narrow the recipe list. The search box filters by name.

### Recipe Card
Each card shows:
- **Preview image** — rendered with the recipe's example data
- **Taxonomy tags** — family, data pattern, primary geom, sample size
- **YAML snippet** — the exact VizFactory plot spec

### Cloning to Blueprint
Click **Send to Blueprint** on any recipe to transplant its plot spec into the Blueprint Architect's active node. The recipe is inserted as a T3 `developer_raw_yaml` node and requires a justification before Apply.

### Difficulty guide
| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Simple | 1–2 aesthetics, single geom, no facets |
| Intermediate | Facets, position adjustments, or secondary statistics |
| Advanced | Multi-layer, custom scales, or complex reshaping required |
